camera_analysis/
├── roi_crop.py          # Land ROI percentage box, mask builder, and background dimming
├── vari_map.py          # VARI = (G - R) / (G + R - B) calculation, color mapping, and legend
├── health_classifier.py # Healthy / Yellow / Brown-Dry pixel classification & threshold evaluation
├── vari_stream.py       # Main controller: ESP32-CAM HTTP capture + OpenCV sliders & display

└── requirements.txt     # opencv-python and numpy

roi_crop.py — Defines and applies the Land ROI (rectangular crop/mask) to isolate your plot/tray/foliage area and exclude background noise.
vari_map.py — Computes VARI ($\frac{G - R}{G + R - B}$) on the cropped region and renders a color-binned VARI map + legend.
health_classifier.py — Classifies vegetation pixels into Healthy / Yellow / Brown-Dry (keeping pale/washed-out foliage out of "Healthy") and evaluates overall status against your customizable Healthy % threshold.
vari_stream.py — Ties all three together with the ESP32-CAM HTTP capture (192.168.4.1) and OpenCV slider controller (cv2.createTrackbar).
